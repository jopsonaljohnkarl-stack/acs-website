Offshore staffing pricing in Australia, explained plainly

Here is how offshore staffing pricing works with ACS, why we do not publish a rate card, and how the calculator gives you a real indicative range in seconds.

Calculate your price

A single published number would be misleading, not helpful

Every engagement is scoped to the role: what the person actually does, the seniority required, and whether the role is full time or part time. A rate card would either be so vague it tells you nothing, or so specific it does not match your actual role.

Pricing is provided on application, based on the position and the level required. That is exactly why we built a calculator instead of a rate card. It reflects the role you actually describe, rather than a generic number that would not match your situation.

How the engagement works

One fee, and you can see exactly what is in it

The commercial model is a single monthly fee per team member. There is no separate recruitment or setup fee in the current model.

The fee covers sourcing and screening, assessments, shortlisting with scorecards, onboarding and payments, administrative support, role design, agreed performance measures and ongoing service oversight.

Five things determine what your role costs

  • The role itself and the skills it needs
  • Seniority: junior, intermediate or senior
  • Full time or part time
  • Hours and time zone coverage required
  • Any systems or certifications the role must hold

None of these are hidden. They are the exact inputs the calculator uses, which is why entering them takes seconds and gives you a real indicative range, rather than waiting for a published figure that would not match your situation anyway.
