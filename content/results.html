ACS offshore staffing results, published only with permission

Here is how ACS offshore staffing results are verified before they ever appear on this page. Nothing here is invented, estimated, or published without the client's explicit agreement.

Most published results in this industry cannot actually be verified

It is common practice across the offshore staffing industry to publish sweeping statistics: percentages saved, client counts, satisfaction scores. Very often these figures cannot be traced back to a real, named source, because they were never meant to be checked.

ACS has chosen a different standard. A case study appears on this page only once the client involved has given written permission to publish their result, using their own account of what happened. We would rather have three real, verifiable results than thirty invented ones, and we think a careful reader can tell the difference regardless of which approach a provider takes.

This means, honestly, that the results section builds more slowly than a page full of generic statistics would. We consider that trade off worthwhile.

What appears here as case studies are approved

As of publication, results are being gathered and confirmed with clients who have agreed to be featured. Each case study that appears here will include the context of the business, what was scoped, what changed, and a genuine account from the client rather than a marketing summary written on their behalf.

The standard every published result is held to

So a visitor to this page can judge the standard for themselves, here is exactly what goes into a case study before it is published.

  • The client's business type and the specific problem that led them to ACS, in their own terms
  • What role or roles were scoped, and why that particular structure was chosen
  • What changed as a result, described by the client rather than assumed or estimated by us
  • Written confirmation from the client that they are comfortable being featured, including how their business is identified

A case study without all four of these elements does not get published, regardless of how compelling a partial version might sound.

Willing to be featured? We would like to hear from you

If you are an existing ACS client with a result you would be comfortable sharing, we would welcome the conversation. Being featured is entirely optional and never assumed, and you retain control over exactly how your business is described.

This is also how the results on this page will genuinely grow over time: through real clients choosing to share real outcomes, not through a marketing team writing hypothetical success stories.

A borrowed statistic is not the same as a verified result

A common alternative to publishing client results is publishing industry statistics instead, figures like average cost savings or typical productivity gains sourced from general research rather than from actual ACS clients. These numbers are not fabricated in the sense of being invented outright, but they say nothing about what any specific client of ours actually experienced.

We have chosen not to use this approach, even though it would fill this page considerably faster than waiting for individual client approvals. An average drawn from a different market, a different provider, or a different set of businesses entirely is not evidence about ACS, and presenting it as if it were would blur exactly the distinction this page is trying to make clear.

If a number appears on this page in future, it will be traceable to a specific, named ACS engagement with that client's permission, not to an industry report we are borrowing credibility from.

What it means if this section is still short

If you are reading this before many case studies have been published, that reflects timing and the deliberate approval process, not a lack of client work. We would rather you judge us on the honesty of this page than on a padded results section built to look more established than it is.

The best available evidence at any point before case studies accumulate is a direct conversation. Ask us who else in your industry we have worked with, and we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot share.

This is admittedly a less convenient answer than a page full of testimonials would be. It is also the honest one, and we would rather you trust the answer than be impressed by the page.

Common questions

Why are there so few, or no, results published yet?

Because every result requires written client permission before it appears, and that approval process takes time. We will not publish a result without it, regardless of how long that leaves this section shorter than a competitor's.

Can I speak with an existing client directly?

This depends on individual client willingness and is arranged case by case, respecting their preference for how much involvement they want in a reference conversation.

Do you track outcomes even where they are not published?

Performance is tracked internally for every engagement, as described on the performance and reporting page. Publishing that data externally is a separate decision requiring the client's agreement.

What if my industry is not represented in the results published so far?

That reflects which clients have approved a case study so far, not which industries we work with. Ask directly on the call which industries we currently support.
