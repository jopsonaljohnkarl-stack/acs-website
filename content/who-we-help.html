Who we help

Offshore staffing support across Australian industries

ACS provides offshore staffing support across a wide range of Australian industries. The common thread is not the sector. It is the work: repetitive administration, coordination and back office tasks that can be documented in a process.

The question is the work, not the industry

We are often asked whether ACS works with a particular type of business. Usually the answer is yes, because the test is simpler than the industry. If a task is done on a computer, follows a process you could write down, and does not need someone physically on site, it can generally be supported by an offshore team member.

What changes between industries is the detail. The systems, the terminology, the compliance paperwork and the rhythm of the week. That is what we scope before anyone starts.

The approach does not change between industries

Whatever the sector, the structure is the same. Our established offshore staffing provider handles sourcing, screening, assessments, onboarding and payments. ACS designs the role around your operation, sets performance measures with you and provides ongoing service oversight.
