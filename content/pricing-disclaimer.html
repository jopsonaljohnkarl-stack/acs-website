1. Indicative ranges only

Any price range shown by the ACS cost calculator is indicative only. It is calculated based on the information you provide and is intended to give a general guide, not a binding quote or offer.

2. Final pricing

A final, exact price is provided only after your requirements are reviewed by the ACS team and confirmed with you directly. Final pricing may differ from the indicative range shown by the calculator, depending on the specific details of the role once fully scoped.

3. No contract formed

Use of the calculator, and receipt of an indicative range or a quote, does not create a binding contract between you and ACS. A contract is formed only when both parties sign a formal service agreement. [Solicitor to confirm this accurately reflects the actual contract formation process.]

4. Factors that may change the final price

  • The specific skills, systems and experience required for the role
  • Final agreed hours and working arrangement
  • Any changes to scope identified during the scoping conversation

5. Questions

If you have any question about how a price range was calculated, or about final pricing, contact us directly rather than relying on the calculator output alone.
