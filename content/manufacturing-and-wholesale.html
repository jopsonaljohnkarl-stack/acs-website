Admin and production compete for the same people

Manufacturing and wholesale businesses run on tight margins, and administrative work often falls to whoever is available, pulling them off more valuable tasks.

  • Order processing backs up during busy periods
  • Purchasing administration and supplier correspondence take constant attention
  • Inventory reporting has to be accurate for planning to work
  • Customer correspondence about orders and delivery queues up

The strain tends to appear as order volume grows past what the current admin team can process, at which point production or sales staff are quietly absorbing the overflow instead of doing their actual job.

Order and purchasing administration runs through defined systems

Order processing, purchasing administration and inventory reporting follow structured workflows in your ERP or inventory system. A team member can be screened and trained against those exact systems before touching a live order.

Production and sales decisions stay with your team, as they should. What moves offshore is the data entry and coordination that currently pulls production or sales staff away from the work they were actually hired to do.

It also means order accuracy does not depend on how busy the floor is. Processing and reporting stay consistent during production peaks, rather than becoming the task that quietly slips when everyone is stretched.

Common questions

Can a team member use our ERP or inventory system?

Yes, once trained on it during onboarding. Screening is done against the specific systems your business runs.

Does the team member make purchasing decisions?

No. The role administers purchase orders and correspondence against decisions and approvals made by your team.

Can this scale during a seasonal production peak?

Yes. Many clients add hours or a second team member temporarily around known peak periods, then scale back afterward.

Does this work alongside our existing admin staff?

Yes, most clients use it to extend existing admin capacity rather than replace it, freeing local staff for higher value work.

How quickly can a team member start on order processing?

Timing depends on your systems and processes. We give a realistic estimate on the scoping call rather than a generic promise.

The clearest early signal this is working is production or sales staff spending measurably less time on data entry within the first few weeks.

There is no minimum order volume to start. Smaller operations often benefit the most, since production or sales staff doing double duty on admin have the least spare capacity to absorb further growth.
