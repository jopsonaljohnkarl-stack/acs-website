Offshore staffing FAQs

Answers to the offshore staffing FAQs Australian businesses ask most often, grouped by topic so you can jump straight to what you need.

How the arrangement is structured

Who employs my offshore team member?

Your team member is engaged through our established offshore staffing provider, which handles sourcing, screening, onboarding and payments. ACS designs the role, sets performance measures with you, and provides ongoing service oversight. You deal with ACS throughout.

Full engagement structure, including the formal disclosure, is covered on the dedicated page linked below.

How your team member is engaged

Is this the same as hiring a freelancer?

No. A freelancer works for multiple clients with nobody employing them and nobody overseeing the work. Your team member is dedicated to your business, sourced and screened against a role scoped with you, with ACS providing ongoing oversight.

What if the arrangement does not work out?

Clear engagement terms and replacement support are provided in the service agreement.

Where is my team member located?

Team members work remotely from a managed environment, to hours aligned with your business, using business-grade equipment and connectivity.

Who do I contact if something needs to change?

Your named ACS contact, during Australian business hours. You do not need to manage the delivery partner directly.

How much this costs, and what is included

Why is there no published price list?

Every engagement is scoped to the specific role, seniority and hours required, so a single published number would either be too vague or would not match your situation. Pricing is provided on application, based on the position and the level required.

What is included in the monthly fee?

Sourcing and screening, assessments, shortlisting with scorecards, onboarding and payments, administrative support, role design, agreed performance measures and ongoing service oversight.

Is there a separate setup or recruitment fee?

There is no separate recruitment or setup fee in the current model. The monthly fee is the commercial model.

How do I get an actual number for my situation?

Use the calculator to select the roles, seniority and hours you need for an indicative range, or book a call to talk it through directly.

How candidates are found and chosen

How are candidates screened?

Skills testing, English assessment and role specific assessments, with results summarised on a scorecard so you can compare candidates on the same basis.

Do I get to choose who joins my team, or am I allocated someone?

You choose. You receive a shortlist with scorecards, interview whoever you like, and decide. If nobody is right, sourcing continues rather than settling for the closest match.

How long does it take to find someone?

This depends on the role and how specific the requirements are. We give a realistic estimate on the scoping call rather than a generic promise.

Can I be involved in writing the role scope?

Yes, and it works best when you are. The scoping conversation is built around your actual workflow, not a generic template.

What happens if the wrong candidate is initially selected?

If a shortlisted candidate does not work out after selection, sourcing continues rather than treating the initial match as final. The goal is the right fit, not simply filling the role quickly, even if that means the process takes a little longer than expected.

What happens after the person starts

What does ACS actually do once the role is live?

We measure the work against the performance measures agreed during scoping, provide regular reporting, and stay involved in the service. This ongoing oversight is the part that separates a managed service from simply supplying a name.

How is performance measured?

Against measures specific to the role, agreed before the person starts, covering things like output, quality and responsiveness relevant to that particular job.

What if the role needs to change after it starts?

Scope adjustments based on real experience are normal, particularly during the first 90 days. This is discussed openly rather than treated as a failure of the original scoping.

Can the same team member take on more responsibility over time?

Yes, that is common as trust and competence are demonstrated, particularly for roles like operations management where authority expands in stages.

What if my business needs change significantly after the role starts?

Scope and hours can be adjusted as your business changes. This is discussed with your named ACS contact rather than requiring a completely new engagement to be set up.

What happens if I want to proceed

What does the first call actually involve?

A conversation about what the work actually is, the real tasks and systems involved, not a sales pitch. We will also tell you honestly if part of the work should stay in Australia.

Is there any obligation after the first call?

None. If it is not the right fit, we will say so, and there is no cost or commitment for having the conversation.

Which industries does ACS work with?

A wide range. If the work can be done remotely and documented as a process, it is generally suitable, regardless of your specific industry.

What roles does ACS currently place?

Admin Assistant, Telesales, Bookkeeper, Operations Assistant, Social Media Manager, Accounting and Finance, Operations Manager, and AI and Automation Specialist.

Can I trial a role before committing long term?

Engagement terms are set out in the service agreement. Raise this directly on the scoping call if a trial period matters to your decision, since it is easier to agree upfront than to renegotiate later.

What happens to my data and access once the engagement ends?

Access is revoked as part of standard offboarding, consistent with the same data security practices applied throughout the engagement. This is worth confirming specifically during scoping if your systems carry particularly sensitive information.
