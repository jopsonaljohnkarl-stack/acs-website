How offshore staff are screened before you ever see them

Here is how offshore staff are screened, step by step, before a shortlist reaches you. Nobody arrives at your interview without going through this first.

Four stages before anyone reaches your shortlist

Sourcing against your scope

Our established offshore staffing provider sources candidates against the role we have scoped with you, not against a generic template.

Skills testing

Practical testing on the specific skills the role needs, not a general aptitude score.

English assessment

Written and spoken English assessed to the standard the role requires, since a bookkeeper and a telesales role need different things.

Role specific assessment

A task or scenario relevant to the actual work, so you are seeing how someone performs, not just what they claim.

You compare candidates on a scorecard, not a gut feeling

Every candidate who reaches your shortlist comes with a scorecard summarising how they performed at each stage. You are comparing people on the same basis rather than reading resumes that all sound similar.

You interview the shortlist and you choose

Screening narrows the field. It does not make the decision for you. You interview whoever reaches the shortlist and you choose who starts. If nobody is right, we go back and source again.
