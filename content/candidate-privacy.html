1. Who this notice covers

This notice applies to individuals who apply for roles connected to ACS through our established offshore staffing provider. It is separate from our main Privacy Policy, which covers client facing personal information.

2. What we collect

  • Application details, including resume, contact information and work history
  • Assessment results, including skills tests, English assessments and role specific evaluations
  • Interview notes and scorecards prepared during the screening process

3. How it is used

Application and assessment information is used to evaluate candidates and present a shortlist to prospective Australian clients.

4. Who it is shared with

Shortlisted candidate information, including scorecards, is shared with the specific Australian client considering the role. It is not shared with other clients or used for any other purpose.

5. Retention

We keep information about unsuccessful applications and assessments only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, then securely delete or de-identify it.

6. Your rights

You may request access to or correction of the personal information held about you by contacting admin@allcommercialservices.com.au.
