What the week looks like

The work that happens after seven

  • The inbox. Triaged to your rules, so what reaches you is what needed to.
  • Quoting and invoicing. Out on time, followed up on a schedule.
  • Bookkeeping basics. Reconciliations and payables kept current rather than caught up quarterly.
  • Scheduling and customer contact. The calls and confirmations that interrupt everything else.
  • Whatever is eating your week. The honest answer, and the one the specification exists to capture.
