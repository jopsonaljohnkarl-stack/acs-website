The screening process

Shortlisted candidates go through skills testing, an English assessment, and a role specific assessment. Successful candidates are presented to an Australian business as part of a shortlist, and the client makes the final hiring decision.

English. Reliability. Attention to detail.

Strong written and spoken English, reliability, and genuine attention to detail matter more than a specific qualification for most of these roles.
