Administration competes directly with billable hours

Legal and professional services firms run on time, and every hour spent on document preparation or billing admin is an hour not spent on client work.

  • Document preparation and formatting takes time from fee earning staff
  • Matter administration, from file opening to closing, needs consistent attention
  • Billing and time entry processing falls behind during busy periods
  • Client correspondence and diary management compete with deadline work

The pressure is structural in this sector. Billable time is the business, and every hour spent on formatting, filing or billing administration is an hour of revenue that did not happen.

Document and matter administration is procedural, not advisory

Formatting, proofreading, file management and billing administration follow defined firm procedures. None of it involves legal judgement or advice, which stays entirely with your fee earners, as it must.

What a dedicated team member removes is the mechanical layer around that judgement, the preparation and administration that currently competes with billable work for the same hours in the day.

It also protects fee earner capacity as the practice grows. Document and matter administration can scale with caseload without requiring another fee earner simply to keep up with the paperwork behind the advice.

Common questions

Can a team member access our practice management system?

Yes, with access limited to what the role requires and confidentiality agreements in place before any access is granted.

Does the team member provide any legal advice or judgement?

No. The role is administrative only. All legal judgement, advice and client direction remain entirely with your fee earners.

How is client confidentiality protected?

Through controlled access, confidentiality agreements and the same data security practices applied across every ACS engagement.

Can this scale across multiple practice areas?

Yes, provided each area's documentation and billing processes are clearly defined during scoping.

How quickly can a team member start on document preparation?

Timing depends on your systems and confidentiality requirements. We give a realistic estimate on the scoping call.

Given the confidentiality obligations in this sector, most firms prefer a staged introduction, starting with lower sensitivity tasks before expanding into matter administration.

There is no minimum firm size to start. Smaller and sole practitioner firms often benefit the most, since there is no existing paralegal or support staff layer already absorbing the administrative load.

If you are unsure whether a task is administrative or requires a fee earner's judgement, raise it on the call. That distinction is exactly what good scoping is for, and we would rather ask than assume.
