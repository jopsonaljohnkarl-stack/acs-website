What happens on the call

  • We ask about the work you are trying to get off your plate, in detail
  • We tell you honestly whether an offshore team member suits it, and if part of it should stay in Australia
  • If it is a fit, we outline what happens next and roughly what timing looks like

Most calls run 20 to 30 minutes. There is no obligation to proceed afterward.
