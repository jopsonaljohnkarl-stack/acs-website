What the week looks like

Capacity that does not disappear in July

  • Workpaper preparation. To your templates and your standard, ready for review rather than for rework.
  • Client chasing. The records nobody sends until asked four times.
  • Data entry and coding. The volume work that consumes a graduate's year.
  • Compliance scheduling. Lodgement dates tracked and clients prompted before they are late.
  • Practice admin. Onboarding, engagement letters and the file management a busy season buries.
