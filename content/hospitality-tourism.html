Common questions

Can a team member use our booking or property management system?

Yes, once trained on it during onboarding, whether that is a channel manager, PMS, or a custom booking tool.

Can this scale up just for our peak season?

Yes. Many clients plan additional hours or a temporary second team member specifically around known peak periods.

Does the team member communicate directly with guests?

Yes, for routine correspondence such as confirmations and standard queries, following your tone of voice and escalation guidelines.

What about complaints or sensitive guest issues?

These are flagged back to you or handled per an escalation process agreed during scoping.

How quickly can a team member start on reservations?

Timing depends on your booking system and processes. We give a realistic estimate on the scoping call rather than a generic promise.
