General admin has no natural owner, so it becomes yours

Every business accumulates administrative work that does not fit neatly into any other role, and by default, that work usually lands with the owner.

  • The inbox is a constant source of interruption and unread messages
  • Documents need preparing, formatting or filing, and it never feels urgent enough to prioritise
  • Customer admin queries pile up between more pressing tasks
  • General admin tasks get done late at night or not at all

None of this work is individually hard. It is the volume and constant interruption that makes it costly, since every small admin task is also a context switch away from whatever actually needed focus.

General admin is defined by process, not location

Email management, document preparation and customer admin all run through systems: your inbox, your document templates, your customer records. A dedicated assistant can be trained on these exact systems and screened against them before handling live work.

What the role needs most is consistency and reliability, someone who processes the inbox and documents the same way every day rather than in occasional catch up sessions. That consistency is what actually keeps admin from piling up.

It also means interruptions get absorbed by someone whose job is to be interrupted for this, rather than by you in the middle of something that needed your full attention.

You are probably ready if this sounds familiar

Most owners reach this point once the business grows past what a single person can handle alongside everything else.

  • Your inbox has messages that have been sitting unread for days
  • Document preparation gets pushed to evenings or weekends
  • Customer admin queries are answered late because there is no dedicated time for them
  • You are doing admin instead of the work that actually grows the business

None of these signs mean you are disorganised. They are simply what happens when general admin has no dedicated owner and defaults to whoever is available, which is usually you.

Scales with the volume of admin the business generates

Most businesses start with email management, since it has the most immediate effect on daily interruptions. Document preparation and customer admin are often added once the inbox is under control.

As the business grows, the same admin assistant can generally absorb more volume before a second is needed, since the underlying tasks do not change, only the quantity.

Some businesses eventually split the role, with one assistant focused on internal admin and documents and another on customer facing correspondence, once both areas are busy enough to justify it.

Can this role handle sensitive customer information?

Yes, under the same confidentiality and data security practices applied across every ACS engagement.

How do I know what gets escalated to me versus handled directly?

You set clear guidelines during scoping, and those guidelines are refined as the working relationship develops.

Can the role expand into more specialised admin over time?

Yes. Many clients start broad and narrow the role toward whichever tasks the assistant is strongest at and the business needs most.

Does this suit a very small business?

Yes. Sole traders and small teams are often the ones who benefit most, since there is no existing admin capacity absorbing the work.
