The engagement model

How your offshore team member is engaged and supported

A fair question, and one worth answering properly. Here is how the offshore staffing engagement works, structured plainly, with each party’s responsibility set out.

Two parties, two jobs, one point of contact

Your offshore team member is an independent contractor engaged through our established offshore staffing provider.

The provider handles sourcing, screening, assessments, onboarding, payments and administrative support. ACS designs the role, sets the performance measures with you and provides ongoing service oversight. You deal with ACS throughout.

Our provider runs the recruitment engine. We run the relationship.

Our established offshore staffing provider handles

  • Candidate sourcing and screening
  • Skills, English and role specific assessments
  • Candidate shortlisting and scorecards
  • Onboarding and payments
  • Ongoing administrative support

ACS handles

  • Understanding how your operation works
  • Designing the role around your workflow
  • Establishing service expectations and performance measures
  • Managing the Australian client relationship
  • Communication and ongoing service oversight

Finding someone is the easy part

There is no shortage of providers who will introduce you to capable people offshore. The introduction is not what determines whether the arrangement works.

What determines it is whether the role was designed around how your business actually runs, whether the expectations were set clearly at the start, and whether anyone is still paying attention in month three. That is the layer ACS adds.

Other providers help you find someone. We help make that person work inside your operation.

Remote, to agreed Australian client hours

Team members work remotely using business-grade equipment and connectivity, with working hours aligned to agreed Australian client hours. Operational support is provided through established remote management processes.

Common questions

Who selects the person?

You do. Our provider sources and screens candidates against the role we scope with you, then supplies a shortlist with scorecards. You interview and you choose.

How are candidates screened?

Skills testing, English assessment and role specific assessments, with the results summarised on a scorecard so you can compare candidates on the same basis.

Are they working on other clients?

No. Your team member works on your business only.

What does ACS actually do after they start?

We measure the work against the performance expectations agreed at the start, keep communication running between you and the team member, and stay involved in the service. This is the part most providers do not do.

What if it does not work out?

Clear engagement terms and replacement support are provided in the service agreement.

Who do I contact if there is a problem?

Your named ACS contact, during Australian business hours. You do not deal with the provider directly.
