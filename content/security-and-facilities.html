What the week looks like

The admin behind a patrol

  • Rostering and shift changes. Rebuilt every time somebody calls in, usually at the least convenient hour.
  • Incident paperwork. Written up while it is fresh, to the standard the client's insurer will want.
  • Client reporting. On time, every period, whatever the week looked like.
  • Compliance records. Licences, checks and training currency, tracked before they expire rather than after.
  • Invoicing from job data. The gap between what was done and what was billed, closed.
