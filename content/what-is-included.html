What is included in your offshore staffing fee

One fee. Here is exactly what is included in your offshore staffing fee, with nothing held back for later.

Everything the fee covers, in plain terms

  • Candidate sourcing and screening, run by our established offshore staffing provider
  • Skills, English and role specific assessments
  • Candidate shortlisting with scorecards, so you compare people on the same basis
  • Onboarding and contractor payments
  • Ongoing administrative support
  • Role design around your operation, run by ACS
  • Agreed performance measures, set before the person starts
  • Ongoing service oversight and communication

There is no separate recruitment or setup fee in the current model

The monthly fee is the commercial model. It covers sourcing through to ongoing oversight, not just the introduction.

The fee is set by the role, not by a price list

Pricing is provided on application, based on the position and the level required. What changes the fee is the role itself: the skills needed, the seniority, and whether the role is full time or part time.
