# ACS block theme

⚠⚠ **EVERY COLOUR AND EVERY TYPEFACE IN `theme.json` IS A PLACEHOLDER.**

The palette is a neutral grey ramp with one placeholder accent, and both font
families fall back to the system stack. **Nothing here is a design proposal.**
The register's People and Brand 2.0 says the logo scope decision comes before
design begins, and 3.0 has no tagline selected. Both rows are empty.

**What is real and considered:** the spacing scale, the type scale, the layout
widths, the template taxonomy and the block gap. Those are structure, they are
not brand, and they will survive whatever the logo decision turns out to be.

**When the logo and tagline land**, the change is: swap two palette entries,
swap two font families, drop the mark into the header part. **The structure does
not move.** That is why it was built in this order.

⚠ `customFontSize`, `custom` colour and `customSpacingSize` are all **false** on
purpose. The copy team writes 46 pages into this theme. Leaving the arbitrary
value pickers open guarantees a page that no longer matches the others, and
nobody finds it until launch.
