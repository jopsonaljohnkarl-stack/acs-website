Described by what they own

Junior

Executes defined tasks within an established process. Supervised closely in the first ninety days.

Intermediate

Owns a workflow end to end. Handles exceptions without escalating them to you.

Senior

Owns outcomes and may coordinate others. Contributes to how the process itself works.

Full time is the standard engagement. Part time is available on selected roles, by discussion. Pricing is provided on application, based on the position and level required.
