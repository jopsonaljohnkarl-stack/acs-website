Your range is a starting point, not your final price

The calculator gives you an indicative range based on the role, seniority and hours you select. It is built to be useful within seconds, not to replace a proper scoping conversation.

Once you submit, your requirements go to the ACS team, not into an automated pricing engine for a final number. You will hear back with an exact scoped price for the role you described, along with everything included.

A range reflects your role. A rate card would not

Every engagement is scoped to the specific role: what the person actually does, their seniority, and whether it is full time or part time. A single published number would either be too vague to mean anything or too specific to match your situation.

The calculator solves this differently to a rate card. It reflects the inputs you actually give it, so the range you see is closer to reality than a generic number would ever be.
