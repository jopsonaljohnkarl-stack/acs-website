You did not start the business to do paperwork

Most owners we speak to are doing two jobs. The one they are good at, and the one nobody else picked up. It usually looks like this.

  • The phone rings while you are on a job, so the call goes to voicemail
  • Quotes go out two days late, and the work goes to whoever answered first
  • Invoices sit unsent, so the money comes in slower than it should
  • Scheduling takes half of Sunday
  • Paperwork gets done at night, because there is no other time

You could hire locally. Most owners in this position have tried. The role is hard to fill, expensive to carry, and often too small to justify a full time wage at Australian rates.

Your offshore team member works to your operation, not to a generic process

An offshore team member works on your business, in your systems, to agreed Australian client hours. The role is built around your workflow rather than dropped in from a template.

Team members work remotely using business-grade equipment and connectivity, with working hours aligned to agreed Australian client hours. Operational support is provided through established remote management processes.

Within a few weeks, most clients find the same things have changed.

  • The phone gets answered, so fewer jobs are lost before you hear about them
  • Quotes go out the same day
  • Invoices go out on schedule
  • The scheduling is done before the weekend, not during it
  • You get your evenings back
