What happens after you apply

  1. 01

    Apply through our provider

    Use the apply button on the listing. Applications are handled by our established offshore staffing provider.

  2. 02

    Complete three assessments

    Skills testing, an English assessment and a role specific assessment. Results are summarised on a scorecard.

  3. 03

    Meet the business

    Successful candidates join a shortlist for the Australian business, which interviews and makes the final decision.

How your application details are handled

If you are shortlisted, your scorecard and application details are shared only with the Australian business considering the role. They are not shared with other businesses or used for any other purpose.
